AI Operations Employee

An AI employee for small businesses. It monitors one business inbox and/or website forms, classifies incoming requests, drafts replies, routes work to the right person, follows up on open items, keeps a durable activity record, and delivers a weekly plain-number summary. It wakes when work arrives and shuts down when its shift ends — never running continuously.

TheAI Operations Employee acts strictly within the authority boundaries defined at setup. Actions outside those boundaries — external messages, spending, account changes, or commitments — require human approval.

Who it is for

US businesses of 1–20 people with recurring inbox work and no need for another full-time specialist. If your team spends too much time on follow-ups, categorisation, and routing — and you want a durable, consistent hand that never sleeps — this is the role designed for you.
