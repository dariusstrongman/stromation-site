The design inputs

Your workflow becomes the job file.

A custom employee is shaped around concrete operating constraints, not a broad promise to automate everything.

Workflow

The recurring trigger, steps, expected result, and definition of done.

Systems and data

The specific tools, knowledge, and records needed for the job.

Authority

Automatic actions, approval gates, forbidden actions, and escalation paths.

Controls

Schedule, budgets, audit requirements, reporting, and failure handling.

Build process

From job definition to measured operation.

Custom work is designed, tested, and reviewed before it is treated as a standing employee.

  1. Discovery

    Map the recurring work, people, systems, bottlenecks, and current failure points.

  2. Design

    Define the objective, capabilities, context, permissions, budget, and reports.

  3. Build

    Connect the approved tools and implement the deterministic controls.

  4. Test

    Run bounded scenarios, including blocked actions, failures, and escalation.

  5. Deploy

    Start with a narrow operating scope and a visible audit trail.

  6. Improve

    Use outcomes and failure history to refine the role without silently expanding authority.
