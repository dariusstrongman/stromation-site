Boundaries

What it will never do.

The current role has explicit limits.

  • Send cold outreach or use email as a growth channel.
  • Invent visitors, rankings, results, or attribution.
  • Spend from a company card or raise its own budget.
  • Hold credentials directly or access systems outside the job.
  • Publish a protected change or make a protected business decision without the required approval.
Week one

A bounded start.

The first week establishes the baseline and produces the first complete operating report.

  1. Day 1, initial audit

    Confirm the website, measurement, goals, authority boundary, and known risks.

  2. Day 3, first findings

    Return the strongest supported findings and any approval requests.

  3. Day 7, first weekly report

    Record the baseline, work performed, cost, measured response, failures, and next step.
