§03

Autonomy you can bound is autonomy you can trust

"Trust us" is not architecture. These are the controls actually running today — the same ones a customer's worker inherits.

Budgets

Company spending accrues against a monthly cap with a daily ceiling enforced in code. Each worker run carries its own hard cost cap. When money runs out, work stops — not quality.

Least privilege

Workers hold no company credentials. The control plane holds them, and a worker gets exactly the tools its job requires — a researcher cannot publish, a builder cannot spend.

Audit

Every session writes an event stream — decisions, tool calls, results, costs. Stromation's own stream is public, live, on Stromation Live.

Independent review

Nothing ships because the model that made it says it's good. Published work must carry dated, sourced claims and its own methodology — enforced by a gate that refuses the page otherwise.

Protected governance

Authority, spending floors, audit requirements and the public/private boundary are constitutionally protected — Sol may propose changes; he cannot make them.

Escalation

Actions that need a human — legal signatures, new spending authority, irreversible commitments — stop and ask. Silence is not consent anywhere in the system.

§06

Experiment 501 learns from 1–500

Autonomy without memory is just speed. Every material decision at Stromation enters a ledger before the outcome is known — then reality reports back.

  1. Hypothesiswhat Sol believes may be true
  2. Evidencefor — and against, in writing
  3. Bounded beta maximum the company will lose to learn
  4. Resultwhat actually happened
  5. Decisioninvest, pivot, or retire
  6. Ledgerforecast vs. outcome, kept forever

Forecasts are compared with outcomes, so confidence is calibrated from history — not rewarded for sounding certain. A failed experiment that was cheap, bounded, and recorded is a success of the system. An expensive surprise is not.

Most AI products wait for instructions. Stromation is the other experiment — what happens when an AI system is given objectives, tools, memory, budgets, and consequences, and is expected to actually operate. The company itself is the first test environment. Everything it learns becomes the workforce it sells. — the operating thesis, constitution v3
