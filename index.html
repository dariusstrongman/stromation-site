Hire an AI employee for a role and an outcome — not another chat subscription.

A Stromation employee is not a prompt box. It holds a role, a memory of your business, its own tools and permissions, a monthly budget it cannot exceed, and a performance record you can read. You give it objectives. It does the work, reports what it did, and tells you what it cost.

Who it is for

Businesses of roughly one to twenty people that have recurring digital work — a website that needs attention every week — but cannot justify another full-time specialist to do it.

The first employee — Growth Operator

One role, done properly, before we add a second. It looks after a company's website as a standing job rather than a project:

  • Monitors the site for breakage and drift
  • Finds content and search opportunities
  • Updates and maintains existing pages
  • Analyses traffic and what moved it
  • Researches competitors
  • Drafts and schedules content
  • Flags outdated information
  • Reports weekly, in plain numbers

What makes it different

Proof of work The same employees a customer hires run Stromation's own businesses. We do not ask anyone to trust a demo. The work is visible, including the parts that go wrong.
It has a budget Every employee has a spending ceiling and a recorded cost per task. You can see what it spent and what it produced, and decide whether that trade was worth making.
The right model per job Cheap models for routine work, stronger ones only where the decision is hard. You hire the role and the outcome. Which model did it is our problem, not yours.
It reports honestly An employee that could not finish says so. A confident report of work that never happened is the failure we design hardest against.

Where we are

Pre-launch, and not taking payment yet. The workforce is being tested on Stromation's own web properties first — the same work, on our own money, before it goes near anyone else's business.

A founding group will open once an employee has held the standard on our properties for long enough to be worth someone else's money. If you want to be told when that happens, write to us.
