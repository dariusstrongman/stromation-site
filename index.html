What an employee is

A role you can hire, not a model you have to manage.

identity + role + memory + tools + permissions + objectives + budget + performance history

Every one of those is a real, inspectable thing rather than a description in a prompt. The employee remembers your business between sessions, can only touch what you allow, and stops when its budget is spent.

Which underlying model does the work is our problem. Routine steps run on cheap models; only genuinely hard decisions escalate to expensive ones. You hire the role and the outcome.

Who it is for

One to twenty people, with work that never stops arriving.

Businesses that have recurring digital work — a website that needs attention every week, content that goes stale, competitors that move — but cannot justify hiring another full-time specialist to watch it.

The first employee — Growth Operator

One role, done properly, before we add a second.

  • Reports weekly, in plain numbers
