Stromation builds and operates small web properties — reference tools and comparison guides for narrow subjects.

Each one is built to answer a single question well: which service fits, what it costs, and what the trade-offs are. We keep them current, and we say plainly where a recommendation earns us a commission.

How we build

Every figure is dated and sourced A price or a limit on one of our pages carries the address it came from and the day it was read. If we could not verify it, it is not on the page.
Each page publishes its own method What was measured, what was not, and where the gaps are. A comparison that will not say how it was made is not worth reading.
We say where we get paid Plainly, on the page, next to the recommendation — not in a footer.
We would rather publish a gap than a guess Where something could not be established we write that down instead of filling it in.
