What happens next

Three steps to a working employee.

Setup takes 2–3 business days from your onboarding call.

  1. Onboarding call — within 24 hours

    Sol emails you to schedule a short call. You confirm the inbox and forms in scope, who work routes to, what is automatic, and what waits for your approval. The written authority boundary is agreed before any access is connected.

  2. Access connected — day 1 or 2

    Once the boundary is agreed in writing, the employee is connected to your inbox. It begins watching, classifying, and drafting inside the scope you approved.

  3. First report — within 3 business days

    You receive the first report: what arrived, what was handled, what waited for you, what it cost, and what to adjust. The $199/month subscription begins on the day the employee goes live.
