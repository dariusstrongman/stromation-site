The job

Work that repeats, with one owner.

The role owns the recurring operational traffic of a small business and reports what it found, did, cost, and could not finish.

Watch

Inbox and forms

Monitor one business inbox and/or intake forms, classify every request, and extract the data that matters.

Move

Replies and routing

Draft replies, route work to the right person, and follow up on unfinished items.

Record

Records and reports

Keep an activity record and produce daily or weekly reports. Anything outside its authority is escalated to you.

Operating model

Event-driven. It wakes when work arrives.

The employee does not sit in a loop thinking. A new email, a form submission, or a follow-up deadline wakes it; it does the bounded job, records it, and goes quiet again. A quiet day is recorded as a quiet day — the report says so.

Authority

Automatic where safe. Approval where it matters.

The boundary is written down before work starts.

Automatic

  • Reading and classifying incoming mail and forms
  • Sorting, extracting, and record-keeping
  • Drafting replies for your review queue
  • Internal routing and follow-up reminders
  • Daily/weekly reporting

Requires your approval

  • Important external messages
  • Any spending
  • Account changes
  • Commitments made on your behalf
  • Anything outside the written job
Onboarding

A bounded start.

Onboarding establishes the written boundary and ends with the first report.

  1. Fit and authority review

    Confirm the inbox/forms in scope, who work routes to, what is automatic, and what waits for approval. Fees and terms are agreed in writing.

  2. Connected and watching

    The employee starts monitoring, classifying, and drafting inside the agreed boundary.

  3. First report

    What arrived, what was handled, what waited for you, what it cost, and what to adjust.
