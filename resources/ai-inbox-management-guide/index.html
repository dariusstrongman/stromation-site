How it works

Three steps: classify, draft, approve.

The AI Operations Employee monitors one business inbox for new messages. Incoming messages follow predictable patterns — inquiries, bookings, vendor updates, complaints, and internal notes.

Each message is classified by type: inquiry, vendor, booking, complaint, or internal. The system extracts key details including sender, date, subject, and action requested.

Once classified and enriched, the AI drafts a reply following your written rules. Routine messages in pre-approved categories send automatically; anything new or outside the boundary holds for your approval before sending.

Authority model

What the AI handles automatically versus what requires your approval.

Runs automatically

  • Pre-approved message categories
  • Routine inquiries with standard answers
  • Follow-ups on completed threads
  • Scheduling confirmations within approved windows

Requires your approval

  • New contacts outside existing categories
  • Commitments involving spend or timing
  • Out-of-scope requests or exceptions
  • Complaints or escalations

Never does

  • Spends money or processes payments
  • Makes promises outside its rules
  • Contacts parties not approved by you
  • Modifies authority rules
Weekly report

Plain-number summary sent to your inbox each Monday.

The AI Operations Employee delivers activity reports as plain-number summaries through your inbox on a scheduled basis. Each weekly report includes:

  • Messages handled (total processed by the AI)
  • Category breakdown (how many fell into each classification)
  • Average response time (from incoming message to sent reply)
  • Escalation count (messages held for your approval)

At the end of the week you'll know exactly how your AI employee spent its time and what requires your attention.
