What the AI Operations Employee Actually Does

Business professionals receive an average of 126 emails per day, according to the Radicati Group Email Statistics Report 2024–2028. An AI Operations Employee classifies, extracts, drafts, and reports on exactly those patterns, and escalates anything that requires human approval.

The authority boundary

What always requires your approval.

Everything outside these categories is handled automatically. Everything inside them triggers an escalation alert.

  • •Any spending or invoice approval above the threshold you set.
  • •Account changes or new service signups under the business name.
  • •External commitments on behalf of the business — promises, guarantees, or fees.
  • •Anything outside the patterns it was configured to recognize.

How the rules work

You write the rules. It follows them. Always.

The AI Operations Employee is not a chatbot that guesses or hallucinates. It operates strictly within the authority boundary you define at setup. Those rules are stored and durable — they survive staff changes, busy weeks, and holidays.

This is the part that changes nothing. The rules you set on day one are the same rules on day 365, unless you update them.
