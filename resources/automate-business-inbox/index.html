What it handles

The AI Operations Employee: classify, draft, route, follow up, report.

classify

Classify

Every message tagged by intent: inquiry, vendor update, scheduling, billing, or follow-up.

draft

Draft

Automatically writes responses based on your standard answers and brand language.

route

Route

Routes requests to the right person, adds tasks to your workflow, or logs them for review.

follow

Follow up

Monitors open threads and sends automated follow-up emails within your authority boundary.

report

Report

Produces a plain-number summary: messages received, handled, escalating, open items.

What it escalates

Only what you ask for — spending, account changes, commitments.

Safe reading, sorting, and reporting happen automatically. Anything involving external money, account access, or binding commitments pauses for your explicit approval.

You decide the triggers at setup: invoice amounts, account modification types, or partner names. The employee learns your business and escalates only what matters.

Authority

You set the rules. It works inside them.

The authority boundary is adjustable after deployment. Start with all follow-ups for your review; later, let the employee send routine ones automatically — no rebuild required.

The rules you write on day one survive staff changes and busy weeks. This is the part that stays constant while your team scales.
