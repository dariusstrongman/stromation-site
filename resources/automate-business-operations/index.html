Workload measurement

Measure before estimating.

Count the requests handled each week and measure the average minutes of human work per item. Weekly requests multiplied by average minutes per request, divided by 60, gives a baseline number of human hours.

Repeat the same measurement during the pilot and compare the observed result. This page does not promise a workload reduction or dollar savings figure.
