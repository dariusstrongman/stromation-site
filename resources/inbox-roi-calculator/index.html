How this was measured

The calculator estimates the annual cost of manually handling incoming email and the potential savings with an AI Operations Employee. Formula: emails/day × minutes/email × 5 days/week ÷ 60 = hours/week; hours/week × 52 × hourly rate = annual manual cost; AI year-1 cost = $250 setup + $199 × 12 months = $2,638; savings = annual manual cost minus AI annual cost; payback in days = $250 ÷ (monthly savings ÷ 30.44). Default inputs: 30 emails/day at 4 min/email and $50/hr produce 10 hours/week and roughly $13,000 annual manual cost. Pricing: verified against the AI Operations Employee founding pilot at www.stromation.com/operations-employee/ on 2026-08-25.
